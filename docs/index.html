Methods for Structural Microeconometrics

Welcome to this course! Find details on the syllabus here.

Here is a link to the git repo for this course, which by cloning is probably the simplest way for you to get access to the data, code, and assignments. I will continue to add materials throughout the course. You may prefer to selectively download these materials.

Topics

The course follows three parts of the online textbook:

  1. Identification. Key readings: Keane (2010), Angrist and Pischke (2010), Marschak (1953), Heckman and Vytlacil (2007).
  2. Discrete Choice, static and dynamic. Key readings: Rust (1987), Hotz and Miller (1993), Aguirregabiria and Mira (2002), Arcidiacono and Miller (2011).
  3. Unobserved Heterogeneity. Key readings: Arcidiacono and Miller (2011), Bonhomme and Manresa (2015), Kasahara and Shimotsu (2009), Berry and Compiani (2023).

Assessment

Assessment is based solely on the class project, which is due at the end of the semester. The project asks you to pose a research question, write down a model that can answer it, argue that the model is identified, and demonstrate with Monte Carlo simulations that your estimator recovers the answer. See the project page for full details.

The project should be submitted as a single PDF by email.

Suggested Exercises

The exercises below mirror the problem sets from previous years of this course. They are for self-guided learning only and will not be assessed. Working through them is the best way to build the skills you will need for the project, and I am happy to discuss them in office hours. All of them can be found in the online textbook.

Topic Exercise
Difference-in-differences and the labor supply elasticity Exercise: Mr Straw Man
Identification of the labor supply model with instruments Exercise: Labor Supply IV
Solving and estimating a dynamic discrete choice model Exercise: Dynamic Labor Supply
Unobserved types and the EM algorithm Exercise: EM with Types
Hidden Markov models and the forward-back algorithm Exercise: HMM with Missing Outcomes
CCP estimation with welfare time limits Exercise: CCP Estimation with Time Limits

Reading List
